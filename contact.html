COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is your average delivery time?

Our average delivery time across Lahore, Karachi, and Islamabad is 30 to 40 minutes. All food travels in thermal insulated boxes to stay hot and crispy.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept Cash on Delivery (COD), JazzCash, EasyPaisa, and major Visa/Mastercard debit and credit cards.

Is your food 100% Halal?

Yes, absolutely! All our meats, oils, and ingredients are strictly 100% Halal certified and sourced daily from reputable suppliers.

Can I customize my burger or wrap?

Yes! Simply add your instructions (e.g. extra sauce, no pickles, extra cheese) in the "Special Instructions" box on the Cart page.
